# RareChain Helper: User Manual

This manual explains how a helper or driver operates the application, using simple English and the English button names. You can switch the app to Tagalog. Some forms depend on settings chosen by your administrator, so complete the fields actually shown on your screen.

For a short explanation of the application, read the [Simple Introduction](HELPER_APP_OVERVIEW.md).

## 1. Open the application and sign in

1. Connect your phone to mobile data or Wi-Fi.
2. Open the application link supplied by your supervisor in your phone's browser. The helper sign-in page is `/driver/login` on your company's application website.
3. Check that the page says **RareChain Helper** and **Helper sign in**. Driver accounts use this page too.
4. Enter the phone number or email registered to your account.
5. Enter your password. Use the eye button if you need to check what you typed.
6. Tap **Sign in**.
7. Open **Profile** and check that the displayed name and role are yours.

Use your own account so the crew's actions are recorded under the correct person. If the profile is wrong, contact dispatch before recording work.

### If you cannot sign in

| Message or situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong phone number or password | Check the registered number or email and re-enter the password. |
| Too many tries | Wait a few minutes before trying again. |
| Account is off / No access | Ask dispatch or the administrator to check your account access. |
| No signal | Restore your internet connection, then try again. |
| Forgot password | Tap **Forgot password? Call dispatch**. Ask dispatch for a reset link, open it on your phone, choose a new password, and sign in again. |
| No dispatch number is shown | Ask your supervisor for the correct contact number. |

## 2. Choose your language

On the sign-in page, use **EN / TL** to change between English and Tagalog.

After signing in:

1. Tap **Profile**.
2. Find **Language**.
3. Tap **English** or **Tagalog**.

The choice is saved in that browser when browser storage is available.

## 3. Understand the main navigation

Use the bottom menu to open **Today**, **Acknowledge**, **History**, or **Profile**. A trip opens its own working screen; use **Back to today** to return to your assignments.

Open **Alerts** when you need to read notifications. A number beside an alert or acknowledgement control indicates unread alerts or orders still needing acknowledgement.

### What Today shows

The **Today** screen shows your assigned trips, progress, and available dispatch alerts. A trip card can show the truck plate, trip number, order count, weight, route information, crew contacts, and a main action button.

If the same truck has multiple trips, use the trip number and orders to identify the correct one. For example, **Trip 1 of 2** and **Trip 2 of 2** are separate runs.

Tomorrow's work may appear in a separate section. You can acknowledge tomorrow's orders when available; start that trip on its assigned day. Overdue work may also appear, so check the date before acting.

If you see **No trips today**, check your connection and ask dispatch whether work has been assigned to your staff account.

## 4. Check and acknowledge the orders

Acknowledging means: **I checked these assigned orders and have them for this trip.** It is separate from starting the trip and recording delivery.

1. Open **Acknowledge**, or use the acknowledgement action on your trip.
2. Check the date. Select **Today** or **Tomorrow** as appropriate.
3. Find the correct truck and trip.
4. Read each order's reference, customer or destination, items, and quantities shown.
5. Compare the information with the actual goods and documents. A supplier pickup can have different instructions from a customer delivery; follow the order type shown.
6. If something is missing or incorrect, contact dispatch or use **Problem with an order?** before confirming an incorrect load.
7. When everything is correct, tap **I have all N orders**. For one order, the button says **I have the order**.
8. If **Quick check** opens, answer the required checklist fields and tap **Confirm**.
9. Check the acknowledgement status.

**Acknowledged** means the action is recorded. **Waiting to send** means it is still saved on your phone and needs to reach the server.

Your driver and helper share this acknowledgement. If the app says another crew member already completed it, check the recorded result and continue from the current stage.

Past dates in the acknowledgement screen are **view only**.

## 5. Start the trip

1. Return to **Today** and open the correct trip.
2. Check the truck plate, trip date, orders, and crew.
3. Finish the required acknowledgement first.
4. When leaving the warehouse, tap **Start trip**.
5. Read the confirmation and tap **Start**.
6. Allow location access if your phone asks for it.
7. Check that the app moves to the delivery stage or displays a saved action waiting to send.

If a later trip says **After trip 1**, finish the earlier run first. If the main button is unavailable, read the trip status and any message; the required previous stage may still be incomplete or sending.

## 6. Choose the next stop and navigate

1. In the active trip, find **Choose your next stop**.
2. Review the pending stops and their order details.
3. Tap **Deliver this next** for the stop your crew will visit.
4. Tap **Navigate** to open the available map destination.
5. Check the address against the order and coordinate the route with your driver.

The current app lets the crew select pending stops in any order. You can use **Choose another stop** to change your choice. Selecting a stop does not record arrival or complete a delivery.

Use **View Route / Navigate** when you want to open the available overall route. If navigation is unavailable or the address looks incorrect, contact dispatch rather than guessing the location.

### Calling someone

Use **Call driver**, **Call customer**, or a named crew call button when a valid phone number is available. The button opens the phone's calling function. If no contact button is available, ask dispatch for the number.

## 7. Record your arrival

1. Arrive at the selected customer, warehouse, or pickup location.
2. Check that the screen shows the correct stop.
3. Tap **I'm here**.
4. Allow the app to obtain your location.
5. Check for the recorded arrival or a waiting-to-send message.

If the app says **No GPS**, turn on phone location and check location permission for the browser. If it says you are far from the customer, confirm the selected order and address. Tell dispatch if the order's map location is incorrect.

Arrival records that you reached the stop. You still need to record the delivery result.

## 8. Record a fully completed delivery

After handing over the correct goods and completing the necessary customer documents:

1. Open the reached stop and tap **Delivered**.
2. Check the customer and order shown in the form.
3. Review the **Invoice / DR number**. Enter or correct it when editable. This field accepts digits only; keep any leading zeroes from the document.
4. If the number is already filled in and locked, check it against the document. Contact dispatch if it is wrong.
5. If a proof photo is requested, tap **Take photo** and allow camera access if asked.
6. Check that the photo is clear and belongs to this delivery. Use **Retake** when necessary.
7. Complete any additional required fields.
8. Tap **Delivered** to save the completed result.
9. Check that the stop's result changed or that the action is waiting to send.

A required photo must be added before the form can be submitted. Photo availability and required fields depend on the active company settings. If **Photo not available** appears, follow the remaining form instructions and tell dispatch if proof is required for your work.

For a **Transfer Order**, the form shows a **TO number** instead of a normal invoice field. Purchase-order work can show a **PO number** and supplier pickup information. Check those instructions carefully; ask dispatch if you are unsure what movement the stop represents.

After saving, choose the next pending stop and repeat the arrival and result steps.

## 9. Record a partial or unsuccessful delivery

Use the actual result. A partially delivered order means some goods were accepted; an unsuccessful delivery means the delivery did not happen.

1. Open the reached stop's **Delivered** form.
2. Tap **Not fully delivered?** when this option is available.
3. Select **Partial** or **Not delivered**.
4. Choose the reason that matches what happened.
5. If you select **Other**, type a clear explanation.
6. Complete the document, photo, and other fields required by the form.
7. Tap **Save**.
8. Check the result and tell dispatch what goods or documents are returning.

Examples of partial-delivery reasons include missing items, damaged items, customer refusal of some goods, and wrong items. Examples of unsuccessful-delivery reasons include a closed store, absent customer, wrong address, refusal, or missing payment / PO.

Example: The customer accepts eight boxes and refuses two damaged boxes. Choose **Partial**, select the matching reason, and explain the quantities to dispatch. Do not mark the whole order as fully delivered.

If the option you need is missing, contact dispatch. The choices available depend on the configured delivery form.

## 10. Report a problem during the trip

Use **Problem?** to tell the office about something that needs attention.

1. Tap **Problem?** in the trip screen, or use the order problem action.
2. Select the affected order, or choose **Whole trip** if the problem affects the run.
3. Choose the category: customer not there, wrong address, damaged goods, missing items, late / traffic, truck problem, cold / reefer problem, or other.
4. Add a useful note. Explain what happened, where you are, and what help you need.
5. Add a photo if available and useful.
6. Tap **Send**.
7. Check whether the report was sent or saved without signal.

Example note: "Customer at Stop 2 is closed. We arrived at 10:15 AM. Please confirm whether we should return later."

**Reporting a problem does not finish the stop.** You must still record the appropriate delivery result when the situation is resolved or dispatch gives instructions. For an urgent operational problem, call dispatch as well.

## 11. Return to the warehouse and end the trip

1. Check that every stop has its actual result recorded, including partial and unsuccessful deliveries.
2. Return to the warehouse with the remaining goods and documents.
3. Once you are back, tap **End trip, back at warehouse**.
4. Read the confirmation and tap **End trip**.
5. Check the trip summary and invoice status.

If the end-trip button is unavailable, review the pending stops and unsent actions. Contact dispatch if you need to return with unresolved work.

Do not end the trip while still travelling back. The button records that the crew has returned.

## 12. Give the signed invoices to the Logistics Officer

After ending the trip, the screen instructs you to give the signed invoices to the Logistics Officer.

1. Gather the signed invoices and delivery documents for the trip.
2. Physically hand them to the Logistics Officer.
3. Explain any missing document, returned goods, partial delivery, or unsuccessful delivery.
4. Check the app's invoice list for the officer's updates.

The helper does not fill in a separate invoice-handover form in the current trip screen. The officer checks the documents through the office workflow.

| Status | What it means / what to do |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting | The document is waiting for checking. |
| Received | The officer marked the document received. |
| Missing | Follow up with the officer about the missing document. |
| Wrong | Check the document or number with the officer. |
| Trip closed | The trip has reached its closed state after officer checking. |

**Trip done** and **Trip closed** are different stages. After the crew returns, officer checking can still be outstanding.

## 13. Work when the internet is weak or unavailable

The app can display previously saved trip data and queue supported actions on the same phone. It does not guarantee that a new sign-in, unseen assignment, or every screen will work without internet. Open your work while connected before leaving.

### Understand the messages

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Offline, showing saved data | You are viewing a saved copy. It may not include the latest dispatch changes. |
| No signal - saved / Saved, will send | The action or photo is stored locally and still needs sending. |
| Waiting to send (N) | N actions are waiting on this phone. |
| Sending | The app is trying to send the saved work. |
| Sent | The server confirmed the action. |
| Not sent (N) | Some actions need attention; open the status panel. |

### Send your saved actions

1. Restore mobile data or Wi-Fi.
2. Open the same application in the same browser on the same phone.
3. Tap **Waiting to send** or **Not sent** to open **Saved on this phone**.
4. Tap **Send now**.
5. If an item still fails, read its message and use **Retry** when appropriate.
6. If it keeps failing, contact dispatch with the trip, order, action, and error message.
7. Check for **Everything is sent** before logging out or switching devices.

Keep the browser's saved data until the work is sent. Clearing browser storage, using a different browser, or switching phones can make locally saved work unavailable on the device you are using.

Use **Dismiss** only after confirming that the rejected action should be discarded; it asks you to throw the action away. If dispatch removed an order or trip, check the latest assignment and contact dispatch rather than repeatedly recording work against it.

If another crew member already completed an action, the app may say **Already done by ...**. Review the shared trip state before trying again.

## 14. Read alerts and assignment changes

1. Open **Alerts** or the dispatch alert banner.
2. Read the update and check which trip or order it concerns.
3. Open the relevant current trip when needed.
4. Use **Mark all read** after reviewing the alerts, if appropriate.

An order or trip removed by dispatch can no longer be worked on through your old assignment. If the change conflicts with goods already on the truck, call dispatch for instructions.

## 15. Review previous trips

1. Tap **History**.
2. Choose **This week** or **This month**. These controls request the recent 7-day or 30-day range.
3. Find the date and truck.
4. Tap the trip to review its details, results, and available proof.
5. Tap **Load more** if older entries are available in that range.

Historical trips are for review. Contact dispatch if you find an incorrect recorded result.

## 16. Manage your profile and password

**Profile** shows your name, role, warehouse, phone number, today's truck, and relevant crew information when available. Ask dispatch or the administrator to correct account or assignment details.

To change your password:

1. Open **Profile** and tap **Change password**.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Enter a new password of at least eight characters.
4. Enter the same new password again.
5. Tap **Save new password** and check for confirmation.

To log out:

1. Check that all actions and photos have been sent.
2. Open **Profile** and tap **Log out**.
3. If warned that actions have not been sent, cancel logout and send them first.

## 17. Common problems and simple fixes

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| My trip is missing | Check the signed-in profile, date, and connection. Ask dispatch to confirm the crew assignment. |
| Wrong truck or driver | Contact dispatch before starting. |
| I cannot start the trip | Check acknowledgement, assigned date, earlier trips, and any message on the screen. |
| I cannot record delivery | Record arrival first, then complete the required form fields. Read any error shown. |
| Invoice number will not accept letters | The invoice / DR input accepts digits only. Ask dispatch how to handle a document that does not match. |
| Camera does not open | Check browser camera permission and try **Take photo** again. |
| A required photo is missing | Take the requested photo and submit again. If the photo feature is unavailable, contact dispatch. |
| GPS is missing or inaccurate | Turn on location, allow browser location access, and check the selected address. |
| A call button is missing | The contact may have no usable phone number. Ask dispatch. |
| An action says Not sent | Open the saved-actions panel, read the error, restore internet, and retry. |
| The trip says it is no longer yours | Dispatch may have changed the crew or removed the trip. Check Today and contact dispatch. |
| Trip is done but not closed | Hand over the documents and follow up on officer checking. |

When asking for help, give your name, truck plate, trip date, order reference, the button you tapped, and the exact message shown. Never send your password.

## 18. Daily checklist

### Before leaving

- [ ] I am signed in under my own account.
- [ ] My phone has charge, internet, and the required permissions.
- [ ] I checked the correct truck, trip date, driver, orders, goods, and documents.
- [ ] I acknowledged the correct orders.
- [ ] I started the trip when leaving the warehouse.

### At each stop

- [ ] I selected the correct stop and recorded arrival.
- [ ] I checked the goods and documents with the customer or receiving person.
- [ ] I recorded the actual result and completed the required proof.
- [ ] I reported any problem and coordinated with dispatch when needed.

### After returning

- [ ] Every stop has its actual result recorded.
- [ ] I ended the trip after returning to the warehouse.
- [ ] I handed the signed documents to the Logistics Officer.
- [ ] I explained any missing documents or returned goods.
- [ ] I checked that saved actions and photos were sent.
- [ ] I followed up on any missing or wrong invoice status.
