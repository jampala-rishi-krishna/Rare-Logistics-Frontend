# RareChain Helper: Simple Introduction

RareChain Helper is the mobile-friendly application that delivery helpers and drivers use to manage their assigned work in IntelliFleet. It helps the crew check orders, record deliveries, report problems, and see whether the Logistics Officer has checked the returned invoices.

This introduction describes the application in the current project source. Screen wording may appear in English or Tagalog, depending on the language selected.

For instructions on each task, read the [Helper Application User Manual](HELPER_APP_USER_MANUAL.md).

## Who uses it?

| Person | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| Helper | Checks the assigned orders, records arrival and delivery results, takes proof photos when requested, and reports problems. |
| Driver | Uses the same application for assigned trips and coordinates with the helper. |
| Dispatch / planner | Assigns the truck, crew, and orders through the office platform and handles assignment problems. |
| Logistics Officer | Checks the returned invoices through the officer's screens. |

Helpers and drivers see work assigned to their crew. Their actions update the same trip. For example, if the helper acknowledges the orders, the driver does not need to acknowledge those same orders again.

## What can you do?

- See the truck, crew, orders, weight, and available route information for your trips.
- Check and acknowledge the orders assigned to the truck.
- Start a trip when leaving the warehouse.
- Choose which pending stop to visit next.
- Open navigation and record arrival at a stop.
- Record a full delivery, partial delivery, or unsuccessful delivery when those choices are available.
- Enter the requested document number and attach proof photos when requested.
- Report customer, goods, traffic, truck, or refrigeration problems.
- End the trip after returning to the warehouse.
- Check invoice receipt status and view past trips.
- Change the language or password and call dispatch when a contact number is available.

## The main screens

| Screen | Simple explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Today | Your assigned trips and today's progress. Tomorrow's assignments can also appear for acknowledgement. |
| Acknowledge | Check the orders and confirm that you have them. A date selector lets you review other available dates. |
| Trip Details | Work through the trip: Load, Deliver, and Return. The main button changes as you complete each stage. |
| History | Review previous trips and their recorded results. |
| Profile | Check your account, truck, crew, language, password, and dispatch contact. |
| Alerts | Read available notifications, including updates from dispatch. |

## A normal working day

1. Open the application link provided by your supervisor and sign in.
2. Check the truck, driver, trip date, and assigned orders.
3. Compare the orders with the actual load, then acknowledge them.
4. Start the trip when leaving the warehouse.
5. Choose a stop, travel there, and tap **I'm here** after arriving.
6. Record the delivery result and complete the requested proof.
7. Repeat for the remaining stops.
8. Return to the warehouse and end the trip.
9. Give the signed invoices to the Logistics Officer.
10. Check that saved actions have been sent and follow up on any invoice problems.

## Words used in the app

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Trip | One assigned run containing orders for a truck and its crew. The same truck may have several trips. |
| Stop | A place or order that needs a recorded visit or delivery result. Check the individual order even when several orders share an address. |
| Acknowledge | Confirm that you have checked and have the assigned orders. It does not mean the goods have been delivered. |
| SO | Sales Order: an order for goods. |
| DR | Delivery Receipt: a delivery document. |
| TO | Transfer Order: goods moving between warehouses. |
| PO | Purchase Order: work that may involve collecting goods from a supplier. |
| Proof of delivery | The photo or other information requested to support the delivery record. |
| Sync / send | Transfer an action saved on your phone to the platform. |
| Closed | The trip has reached its closed state after officer checking. Ending the trip alone does not mean it is closed. |

## What you need before starting

- Your own enabled helper or driver account and password.
- The correct application link from your supervisor.
- A charged phone with internet access for signing in and loading assignments.
- Location access for recording arrival and trip actions, and camera access for requested photos.
- The dispatch contact number and the delivery documents for your work.

The app can save supported actions on the phone during a connection problem. A saved action still needs to be sent. Read the manual's offline instructions before relying on this feature.
